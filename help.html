Sourcing

Use the search bar on the home page or go to Shop. Filter by category (Electric Bikes, Batteries, etc.). Open a product to see price range, MOQ, supplier and details.

Minimum Order Quantity — the smallest number of units the supplier will sell. You must order at least the MOQ shown on the product page.

Verified (and Gold) suppliers have been reviewed on our platform. Prefer these when sourcing for safer trade.

Request for Quotation (RFQ)

On a product page click Get Quote, or go to Request Quotation. Fill quantity, target price and message. The supplier will reply with a quote. No payment is required to request a quote.

Usually within 1–2 business days. You will see the reply in Messages or we may contact you by phone/email using the details you provided.

Place Order

1. Open a product → Place Order
2. Enter name, phone, address and quantity
3. Submit → you get an order number
4. Wait for payment instructions in the order chat

Go to Orders (bottom menu or Account). Search by order number or the phone number you used when ordering.

Order Payment

Payment is manual. After you place an order, we send bank account details in the order chat. Transfer the amount, then upload your payment receipt (photo or PDF) in the same chat. We confirm and update the order status.

Open your order (Orders → open order, or Messages). Use the image/attachment button in the chat to upload a photo or PDF of the receipt.

Not at the moment. All payments are confirmed manually after bank transfer and receipt upload for security and flexibility.

Tracking & Messages

Pending — waiting for payment instructions / payment
Confirmed — payment received
Processing — order being prepared
Shipped / Pickup — ready or on the way
Delivered — completed

Open the order from Orders or find it under Messages. You can send text, photos and PDFs. Our team replies from the backend in the same chat.
